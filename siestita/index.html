Lessons

  • One idea per clip. The 9s home clip did 452,705 views. The 49s montage did 350.
  • Watch-through beats concept. The solo dance held 95% average watch and drove most of the follows. The same dog dancing with a cat held 32.9% watch and did 10,884 views.
  • The series format revived growth. 7-day views fell from 494k to 14k. Then “What if dachshunds ran the office?” did 28,163 views, a 10.28s average watch and 13.15% engagement, against 313–544 views for one-off concept reels the same week.
  • The shop doesn't convert from views. 780,408 views in 31 days. Shop posts stayed at 76–174.
